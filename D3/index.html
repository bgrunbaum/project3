How much has the sea surface warmed?

Per-cell linear trend of annual-mean SST, fitted over 1850–2014

Scroll or pinch to zoom · drag the map to pan · double-click or press Reset zoom to start over

Adjust the threshold to highlight areas warming at or above that rate. Cells below it fade out; the percentage is the share of ocean area (weighted by latitude) at or above it.

land

Data: MIROC6 historical run (r5i1p1f1), sea-surface temperature (tos), on the model's native grid of about 1°×1° cells.
Transformations: Monthly values are averaged into annual means. Land cells are excluded, and the map is cropped to 80°S–63°N, where the model grid lines up with latitude. For each end year, a straight line is fitted through every annual value from 1850 to that year, and its slope is reported in °C per decade. "Total change" multiplies that trend by the length of the window. The global mean and the "% of ocean area" readout weight each cell by cos(latitude), so the smaller high-latitude cells don't count too much. The color scale is fixed across years and clamped at ±0.1 °C/decade (±1 °C for total change).
